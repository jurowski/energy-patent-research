# Cross-Patent Mechanism Synthesis: Energy-Device Corpus

## 1. What the corpus actually shows (honest top-line)

**The dominant real signal is conventional engineering, not anomalous physics.** The overwhelming majority of high-confidence fingerprints describe ordinary permanent-magnet machines, resonant power converters, wireless-power coils, water electrolysers, waste-heat bottoming cycles, and piezoelectric harvesters. The "unconventional efficiency" flavor of the corpus comes from a **numerically small but structurally coherent minority** carrying `claimed_effect` values of `over_unity`, `self_sustaining`, `excess_heat`, or `anomalous_gas_yield` (on the order of ~50 of ~400+ fingerprints).

Three candor caveats that limit everything below:

- **The `category` field is unreliable.** "lenr" contains xerographic toner chemistry (DE102017110936B4, JP6410859B2, US11429032B2), a DNA vaccine (US11419932B2), and lithium batteries (US11735740B2). "solid-state" contains OLED materials and drug-delivery nanoparticles (EP2007435B1). "plasma"/"thermodynamic" contain mass spectrometers (US11515136B2, CN106415777B) and medical catheters. **A large minority of the corpus is not an energy-generation device at all.** I lean on the mechanism fields, not the category tags.
- **Low-confidence fingerprints are near-worthless as evidence.** Titles-only entries (US512340 Tesla coil; US2953750A magnetic cable; JP2022089929A ablation antenna; DE20320031U1 radiation converter; US8909505B2 "blueprint of space") should not anchor any conclusion.
- **The most eye-catching over-unity claims are singletons, not patterns.** Kaplan's vortex "core reactor" (US20160032904A1), Bogdanov's spinning 2D-conductor converter (RU2203518C2), Miller's low-impedance generator (CA2977937A1), Donavan's electret "battery" (BR102013021479A2), and Heyring's hyperhedral-lattice windings (US8909505B2) each appear once, at low/medium confidence. They are noise, not whitespace.

The genuinely interesting structure is (a) a handful of **large conventional clusters** worth mining for real design motifs, and (b) a handful of **rare-but-recurring anomalous clusters** where *independent* inventors across decades and countries converge on the *same* mechanism template.

## 3. Rare-but-recurring combinations — the whitespace

These are the interesting cells: mechanism templates that recur **across independent inventors**, not just one prolific filer. That independent convergence is what distinguishes whitespace from a single crank's portfolio.

**W1 — Casimir / zero-point vacuum-fluctuation → rectifying junction → DC.**
`ambient_em`(mostly) · `semiconductor_junction` · `cavity` · `over_unity` · `couples_to_ambient_field=true`.
Moddel: **US11563388B2, US11837971B2, US11258379B2, US11463026B2**; Bressi (independent): **US20190207537A1, US20130283797A1**; Villalobos: **US20180059704A1**; Sadr: **US11723277B2, US12256641B2**.
*Why interesting:* at least three unrelated inventors independently arrive at the identical topology — an asymmetric Casimir/plasmon cavity feeding a diode-like transport element — as a claimed DC source. It is the most internally consistent of all the anomalous families.

**W2 — Resonant water dissociation for "anomalous gas yield."**
`electrical_pulsed`/`electrical_resonant` · `electrical_lc`(or `acoustic`) · `pulsed_sharp_edge`/`sinusoidal` · `anomalous_gas_yield`.
Meyer: **US4936961** (+ management **US5293857A**); **RU2456377C1, RU2515884C1, RU2409704C1, WO2017004732A1**; **US20210156037A1** (cavity "fusor"); **EP2630089A2** (magnet+acoustic+IR); **CN107985063A**.
*Why interesting:* the "tune an electrical or acoustic resonance to water's alleged molecular frequency to break O–H cheaply" template recurs across the US, Russia, Chile, and Japan over ~30 years with no citation lineage — a genuine independent convergence on a falsifiable claim.

**W3 — Pulsed magnetic-collapse / back-EMF "regauging" recovery loops.**
`electrical_pulsed` · magnet+coil · `self_sustaining`/`over_unity`.
Bedini: **US20020097013A1, US20020130633A1**; Illanes: **WO2023239247A1**; Holcomb: **US10008916B2**; Kemp: **US20150188400A1**; Lee: **US9752558B2**; Theuret: **US20240072624A1**; Farquharson: **US10110109B2**.
*Why interesting:* a very stable template — Hall-timed pulse into a PM rotor, capture the collapse spike, feed it back to "close the loop." Notably it **shades continuously into legitimate engineering**: the *same* back-EMF capture appears in conventional, honestly-scoped patents (**US11863096B2**, **US9716424B2**, **JP7587520B2**). This is the best example of a real motif wrapped in an over-claim.

**W4 — Rotating-field (E×B) collisional plasma "fusion at low energy."**
Wong: **US10269458B2, WO2019143396A2, US20230162878A1**; adjacent: Egely dusty-plasma resonance **US20140126679A1**; Blake ion-beam **US20210217537A1**.
*Why interesting:* Wong's rotate-charged-particles-to-force-collisions idea is filed under *both* "electromagnetic" and "lenr" categories — a single mechanism straddling the taxonomy, and echoed by two unrelated inventors.

**W5 — Metal-hydride LENR excess heat triggered by pulsed/RF/acoustic stimulation.**
Godes: **US20220208399A1, HK40002662A**; Winzeler: **US20250022619A1**; Cook (acoustic): **US20110044419A1**; Sullivan (microwave cavity): **US20170260634A1**; plus the thermally-triggered Rossi/Cox family (see §4).
*Why interesting:* the *stimulation modality* varies (current spikes, PWM magnetic pulses, ultrasound, THz-tuned plasma **US11008666B2** by Letts) while the substrate (H/D-loaded Ni or Pd lattice) is constant. The recurring variable is "how you ring the lattice."

**W6 — Hydrino / catalytic sub-ground-state hydrogen, spanning device types.**
Mills: **HK1245508A1, JP6703581B2, US10443139B2, ES2834965T3, JP2023075281A, AU2026200145A1, CN107710331B**; independent metrology by Klein: **DE102017107122A1** ("mole fraction of hydrino in HHO gas").
*Why interesting:* one prolific inventor (Mills) plus one *independent* inventor who built a **measurement** patent presupposing Mills' species. That's an unusual second-party validation attempt — cheap to probe.

**W7 — Ferroresonant / saturating-core self-sustaining generation (thin).**
**CN104333199A** (rotor-free AC generator, feedback boost), **RU2015148551A** (Curie/Barnett-restored inertial motor), **RO138665A2** (ferroresonant motor-generator).
*Why interesting:* small (3), but a distinct template — a saturating/ferroresonant core in a self-feeding loop — separate from W3's PM-collapse family.

## 5. Ranked candidate device concepts to prototype

Ranked by *strength of independent-inventor convergence* × *how cheaply the core claim can be settled*. For each: motivating patents and the single cheapest test that would **falsify** it.

**Rank 1 — Back-EMF / magnetic-collapse recovery drive (buildable, and the claim is falsifiable in one measurement).**
Motivation: **US11863096B2, US9716424B2, JP7587520B2** (honest versions) vs **US20020097013A1, WO2023239247A1, US10008916B2** (over-unity versions). The recovery hardware is real and worth building; the *net-gain* claim is the thing to kill.
Cheapest falsifying test: **battery-to-battery closed loop with coulomb counting** (two matched cells, integrate amp-hours in vs out over N cycles, same cell chemistry/temperature). If net charge transferred ≤ input within meter error, the over-unity claim (not the recovery function) is falsified. ~$50 in shunts + a logging DMM.

**Rank 2 — Resonant-pulse water electrolyser for "anomalous" HHO yield.**
Motivation: **US4936961, RU2456377C1, RU2515884C1, WO2017004732A1, US20210156037A1**.
Cheapest falsifying test: **eudiometer + inline wattmeter**, compute gas volume per coulomb and compare to Faraday's law (≈7 mL H₂ per amp-minute at STP). Any claim of gas beyond ~100% Faradaic efficiency is falsified directly; resonance tuning can be swept while the ratio is watched. ~$30 (graduated gas column, hall-effect current sensor).

**Rank 3 — Casimir/plasmon-cavity DC harvester.**
Motivation (independent convergence, W1): **US11563388B2, US11463026B2** (Moddel) and **US20190207537A1** (Bressi).
Cheapest falsifying test: fabricate the diode-adjacent nano-gap device, place it in a **grounded Faraday enclosure at thermal equilibrium** (no gradient, no RF), and log open-circuit voltage/short-circuit current with a nanovoltmeter over hours. Sustained DC into a load with no gradient and no external field would be the extraordinary result; its absence falsifies. Fabrication is the cost driver — cheapest honest attempt uses an existing tunnel/Schottky diode against a metallized dielectric (~$200 + lab access). Ranked below 1–2 because the *build* is not cheap even though the *test* is clean.

**Rank 4 — Metal-hydride LENR excess-heat cell with stimulation.**
Motivation (W5): **US20220208399A1, US20250022619A1, US20110044419A1, US11008666B2**.
Cheapest falsifying test: **isoperibolic (or simple flow) calorimetry with a blank control** — identical cell loaded with a non-hydriding metal, same electrical/RF/acoustic drive. Output thermal power minus input electrical power must exceed the blank by more than calorimeter uncertainty to survive. A single afternoon with a Dewar, two thermocouples, and a resistive-heater calibration. ~$150.

**Rank 5 — Ambient-RF / atmospheric "self-sustaining" collector (Tesla lineage).**
Motivation: **US685957, US787412, WO2005055409A2, DE20320031U1**; contrast with the *legitimate* bounded rectennas **CN110112546A, US11936413B1**.
Cheapest falsifying test: **shielded-vs-unshielded A/B**. Measure delivered power, then enclose the collector in a Faraday cage. Honest RF harvesting drops to ~zero (confirming it's bounded ambient flux, not a source); a claim of continued self-sustaining output is falsified by the cage. Essentially free if a screen room or metal box is available.

**Rank 6 — Rotating-field collisional-plasma reactor.**
Motivation (W4): **US10269458B2, WO2019143396A2**.
Cheapest falsifying test: **neutron/activation counting at the claimed operating point** — a moderated ³He or bubble-dosimeter check for fusion products, plus calorimetric input/output. Cheap on the *diagnostic* side (a rented neutron dosimeter) but the reactor build is expensive and hazardous; ranked last on prototyping practicality.

### Bottom line
The corpus's real, mineable value is in the **conventional convergence** — back-EMF/multifilar charge recovery (C1/W3 overlap), Halbach flux concentration, fractional-order WPT compensation, and thermoelectric bottoming cycles — where many independent groups have refined the same engineering. The **anomalous whitespace worth a cheap look** is W1 (Casimir DC) and W3-honest (recovery loops), because there the mechanism is both *recurrently, independently claimed* and *settleable with a single meter*. Everything flagged `over_unity` as a singleton, and every low-confidence/title-only fingerprint, should be treated as absence of evidence, not evidence.
