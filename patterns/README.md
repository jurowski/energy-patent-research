## The Meta-Pattern (corrected)

The original synthesis claimed the most promising devices combine *non-linear +
resonance + pulsed excitation*. The mechanism-level re-read found that recipe in
only **13 of 766 patents** (6 with an anomalous claim) — it was largely a keyword
artifact. The real anomalous signal is **independent-inventor convergence** on a
handful of mechanism templates (Casimir/ambient-EM DC, resonant water dissociation,
back-EMF recovery loops, metal-hydride LENR). See
**[12_revised_experiment_priorities.md](12_revised_experiment_priorities.md)** for
the corrected ranking and the falsification-first experiment set.

## Experiments

The original **29** experiments (patterns 01–09) remain valid as Level-1 /
component-characterization work. The **6 priority experiments** in
[12](12_revised_experiment_priorities.md) are the current headline set, ranked by
*independent-inventor convergence × how cheaply the core claim can be falsified*
(each ~$30–200).
